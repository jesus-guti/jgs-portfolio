# Target company research

**Question:** Which currently-hiring (or evergreen-hiring) **product** companies match Design Engineer / Product Engineer / Senior Frontend, English working language, remote EU or EOR/contract viable from Spain, rare onsite — excluding consultancies and staff-aug?

**Checked:** 2 September 2026. Titles churn; re-open the first-party URL before applying.

**Method:** Official careers pages, first-party job posts, and employer handbooks/blogs. Aggregators used only as pointers. Consultancies, agencies, and staff-aug excluded.

---

## Filter

- Ships its own product.
- English working language (stated on the careers/handbook page or international engineering hiring copy).
- Remote EU, EOR/contractor from Spain, or Spain named on the posting. Rare onsite = optional hubs or yearly offsites, **not** 2–3 mandated office days.
- Role family: Design Engineer, Product Engineer, Senior Frontend (or Staff equivalent with a frontend/product lean).

**Status**

- **Open** — matching IC role live on the source.
- **Evergreen** — no matching IC today; first-party page still invites applications and documents EU/Spain/EOR remote.
- **Confirm-geo** — product + title fit; Spain employment not explicit (ask recruiting before a take-home).
