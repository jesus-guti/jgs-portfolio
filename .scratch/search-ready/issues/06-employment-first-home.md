## Question

For a hiring manager in ten seconds, should Home lead with employment work (rely / Safe) or with Loadzone — and if employment leads, what is the Hero thesis, section order, visual, and whether the employer brand appears?

## Answer

The site pitches a **better employed IC**, not a founder or freelancer.

- **Hook:** Employment work (rely, Safe, Sorolla). Products named; employer brand is not on Home (it lives on LinkedIn / About).
- **Hero thesis:** shipping interfaces in production teams / factory systems. Loadzone does **not** appear in the one-liner.
- **Order:** Hero → Employment work → Personal product (Loadzone) → 1-Week.
- **Visual:** Employment is text. The Streak Cromo stays, in the Personal product block. Do not invent a rely still.
- **Cases before contact:** write **rely v2** only. Safe and Sorolla stay unlinked cards until a loop asks.
- **Loadzone:** remains **Personal product** (owned, live) — not a side project. It is proof of ownership and craft, not the job being applied for.
