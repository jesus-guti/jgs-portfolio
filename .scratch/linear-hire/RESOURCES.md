# Linear hire resources

## Knowledge

- [Job: Senior / Staff Product Engineer](https://linear.app/careers/069c4628-88d7-4e4d-b393-c996fc7f3076)
  Source of truth for title, geo (US + Europe), stack, and “what we’re looking for.” Re-open the day of apply.
- [Readme](https://linear.app/readme)
  Mission story: magical software vs noise, sameness, outdated process. Use for: why they exist, not for mimicking the prose in your cover note.
- [How we hire at Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-hire-at-linear) — Doug Parker, Apr 2026
  Scorecard (craft, judgment, ownership, clarity, fit), stages, compensation on first recruiter call, 3–4 conversations then work trial, 4–6 weeks.
- [Why and how we do work trials](https://www.linear.app/now/why-and-how-we-do-work-trials-at-linear) — Nathalie Alex et al., Dec 2023
  Paid 2–5 days; senior often 5; kickoff / candidate-led check-in / presentation; independent feedback; anything but strong yes is a no.
- [Designing remote work at Linear](https://linear.app/now/designing-remote-work-at-linear) — Karri Saarinen, Oct 2025
  How the job feels: 2–4 person teams, no OKRs/A/B, short specs, Quality Wednesdays, zero-bugs, goalie/triage, yearly offsite, optional hubs (Berlin/NY/SF; JD also mentions London coworking).
- [Output isn’t design](https://linear.app/now/output-isn-t-design) — Karri, Apr 2026
  Closest “how we use AI in design” primary: AI generates form; design is fit to context. Do not pitch yourself as an AI output machine.
- [Design for the AI age](https://linear.app/now/design-for-the-ai-age)
  Workbench metaphor: Linear as structured UI; agents as tools on the bench. Use for: product-engineer talk about AI in the core product.
- [Scaling the Linear Sync Engine](https://linear.app/now/scaling-the-linear-sync-engine) — Tuomas Artman, Jun 2023
  Talk: client sync, scale, API shaped by sync constraints. Understand the *problem*; do not claim you have built this.
- [Sharing Linear’s growth with the people building it](https://linear.app/now/sharing-growth-with-the-people-building-linear) — Karri, Aug 2026
  Tender ~$99M at $2.5B; cashflow positive; $100M+ ARR; agents in 95% of paid workspaces. Use for: company stage and why they are hiring, not for equity math in the first email.
- [Giving our team liquidity (first tender)](https://linear.app/now/giving-our-team-liquidity-through-linear-s-first-tender-offer)
  Linear Infinity: 10-year exercise, refreshers, paid month off. Use if they ask “why stay.”
- [The Story of Linear — Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/linear)
  Secondary interview with Tuomas: sync engine lets product engineers ship features without owning networking. Use as context, not as Linear-official.
