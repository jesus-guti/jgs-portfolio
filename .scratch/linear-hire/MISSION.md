# Mission: Linear Senior Product Engineer

## Why
Jesús is targeting Linear’s [Senior / Staff Product Engineer](https://linear.app/careers/069c4628-88d7-4e4d-b393-c996fc7f3076) posting as the job he actually wants: product UI, full-stack TypeScript, taste, ownership, remote Europe. Getting through their process (and surviving a paid work trial) is the concrete outcome — not “getting better at interviews” in the abstract.

## Success looks like
- Apply as **Senior** (not Staff) with a Home URL that a Linear hiring manager can map to craft, judgment, and ownership in under two minutes.
- Walk a recruiter/HM call in English using Linear’s own language (craft, judgment, ownership, clarity, fit) without pitching Loadzone as the job or hiding factory work.
- Reach a work trial able to lead kickoff, written updates, and a check-in without waiting for a PM script.
- Know what of Linear’s stack he must **understand** (sync, GraphQL, realtime) vs what he must not fake.

## Constraints
- Site is live at `https://jesusguti.dev/`. English applications. Based in Spain.
- Little recent interview volume; first live loops should not be Linear.
- Employment work is industrial (Rely, Safe), not a consumer SaaS like Linear. Loadzone is a personal product with one club.
- Time: search in parallel with a full-time job; work trial would need 2–5 paid days off or weekends.

## Out of scope
- Cloning Linear’s visual language on the portfolio.
- Becoming a GraphQL/MobX/Temporal expert before applying.
- Staff-level claims without evidence of Linear-scale product surface.
- Rewriting the entire site as a Linear case study.
