Readme

Software used to feel like magic; now it is noise, sameness, and 20-year-old process language. Linear is the tool for people who still want to make magical software — speed, taste, a better methodology. You are cast as a creator, not a ticket closer.

linear.app/readme

How we hire

Slope over logos. Doers over talkers. Five attributes. Tight process, paid trial, they discuss money early, they claim to give real feedback. Fit warning: autonomy can feel like no feedback unless you go get it.

how-we-hire-at-linear

Designing remote work

Trust, tiny teams, one roadmap, no OKR/A/B religion, written updates, bug SLAs, craft practice (Quality Wednesdays), constraints, customer requests in-product, goalie for unplanned work. Profitability as freedom. Optional coworking. This is the job description of the culture.

designing-remote-work-at-linear

Work trials

They do not trust interviews to find builders. Trial ≈ real project (example: triage incoming feature requests). You get Slack/Notion/Linear/GitHub/Figma. You lead the middle meeting. Strong-yes culture. Senior length often five days, paid, schedule-flexible including weekends.

why-and-how-we-do-work-trials

AI and design

Careers links “how we use AI in design.” The durable primary is Karri: Output isn’t design (form ≠ fit; AI makes form cheap) and Design for the AI age (Linear as workbench; agents on the bench, not a chat void). The JD still asks you to build AI-powered functionality into the core product — taste plus shipping, not prompt demos.

Sync engine talk

Tuomas: realtime client sync is the platform so product engineers can ship features without reinventing networking, offline, races. Watch the talk. You need the problem statement in your bones. You do not need to reverse-engineer their engine in a take-home fantasy.

Tender offer (2026)

Second tender, ~$99M, $2.5B mark, cashflow positive, $100M+ ARR, 40k+ paying companies, agents in most paid workspaces, hiring because scope grew not because they are a desperate grow-at-all-costs shop. Sharing growth. Know this so you do not pitch “scrappy Series A energy.” They are a profitable standard with a still-small team.
